Our Journey

Milestones & Growth

2023 — The Beginning

Samsung Promo Portal launched with a small team of passionate Samsung enthusiasts in Lagos, Nigeria. Started with just 200 participants and 3 campaigns.

Early 2024 — Expanding Across Africa

Expanded to 15 countries including Kenya, Ghana, South Africa, and Uganda. Reached 3,000 active participants and delivered over 500 Samsung devices.

Mid 2024 — 10,000 Members Milestone

Hit the 10,000 member milestone. Launched the Ambassador program and introduced tiered membership plans. Total rewards value crossed $500K.

2025 — Going Global

Expanded to 45+ countries beyond Africa. Introduced Paystack integration for seamless payments. Community reached 12,500+ active participants.

2026 — The Future

Aiming for 50,000 members, launching exclusive Samsung Galaxy AI campaigns, and introducing mobile app for easier participation and tracking.
