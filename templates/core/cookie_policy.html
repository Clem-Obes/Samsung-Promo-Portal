Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Samsung Promo Portal uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. For more details about how we handle your personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.

1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and supply information to site owners.

Cookies cannot access other data on your device and do not carry viruses or install malware.

2 Types of Cookies We Use

We categorize our cookies into the following types:

Category Purpose Duration Required?
Essential Required for the Platform to function — session management, authentication (CSRF tokens, session IDs), and security Session / 14 days Required
Performance Help us understand how visitors interact with the Platform by collecting anonymous usage data (page views, load times) Up to 2 years Optional
Functional Remember your preferences such as language, region, dark/light mode, and login status Up to 1 year Optional
Marketing Used to deliver relevant promotional content and measure campaign effectiveness Up to 2 years Optional

5 Managing Your Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings. Check your browser's help section for instructions:
  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

  • Google Analytics Opt-out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Network Advertising Initiative: optout.networkadvertising.org

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the Platform. You may not be able to:
  • Log into your account
  • Complete purchases or payments
  • Access personalized content
  • Submit forms securely
